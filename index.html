    The fine print, honestly

    what Clearance won't pretend to know
    01 · live mode

    It can't browse. Yet.

    Apps outside the library are answered from Claude's own knowledge, so every line gets stamped UNVERIFIED. We'd rather say "not published" than invent a date.

    02 · login walls

    Creating the app is a human job.

    Our agent tried all five providers and got bounced by every one at sign-in. It does the paperwork around that step: values, scopes, justifications.

    03 · coverage

    Five apps, not a hundred.

    Five checked against the source beat a hundred guessed. Adding the next app is one data entry.

    04 · freshness

    Rules change on a Tuesday.

    Slack throttled unlisted apps in May 2025 without asking anyone. Every guide shows when it was last checked.

    05 · access

    Live mode needs a Claude login.

    It runs on the viewer's own Claude account, which is why it's free. No login, no live mode. The library works for everyone.

    06 · secrets

    We never see your keys.

    No credentials are stored or asked for. If you paste a client secret into the search box, that's between you and your security team.
