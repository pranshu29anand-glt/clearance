Before your AI agent can connect to someone's Slack or Gmail, you need your own OAuth app with that provider, and some providers make you wait weeks. How we score

The verdict

Set by the worst of two gauges: who can get access, and what review applies. Cleared = self-serve, no wait. Hold = a review of days or an unpublished wait. Gated = weeks, a partner programme, or a paid audit.

The layers

0 What is this app. 1 Category. 2 What you want to do, and for whom. 3 Have we verified it. 4 Seven gauges: access, review, permission level, prerequisites, limits before approval, ongoing duties, agent vs you. 5 The verdict and next action.
